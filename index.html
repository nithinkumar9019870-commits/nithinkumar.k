Greener campus living

Small everyday choices add up. Here are a few ways to make a difference right where you learn and grow.

01 / ENERGY ☀️

Solar energy

Make the most of clean, renewable sunshine. Switch off unused lights and support solar-powered spaces.

02 / WASTE ♻️

Smarter waste

Bring a reusable bottle, sort your recycling, and give single-use plastics a miss when you can.

03 / WATER 💧

Water wise

Use only what you need. Report leaky taps and help keep this precious resource flowing for everyone.
